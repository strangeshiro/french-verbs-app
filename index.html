French Verbs

Learn the 100 most common French verbs — grouped into 10 themes, with English translations and présent conjugations.

  • Two quiz modes for every group: pick the right translation, or fill in “nous ___” with the right présent form.
  • Hear every verb: tap the speaker, or tap any form to hear it with its pronoun — “nous avons”, “j’ai”.
  • Track your progress: learned verbs get a check mark, tricky ones are flagged for review, and each group keeps your best score.

FAQ

Does the app need an internet connection?
No, everything works offline.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device: learned verbs and best scores are kept between launches. To start over, open the menu (⋯) and tap “Reset progress”.

How do I get a better French voice?
The app uses the best French voice installed on your iPhone. For a more natural one, go to Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → French (France) and download a voice marked Enhanced or Premium.
